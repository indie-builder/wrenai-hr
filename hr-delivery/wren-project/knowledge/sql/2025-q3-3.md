nl: 2025年（Q3快照）年假使用率最高的前3个部门？
sql: 'SELECT d.dept_name AS 部门, round(sum(b.used) * 100.0 / sum(b.entitled), 1) AS
  年假使用率

  FROM leave_balances b JOIN employees e ON b.emp_id = e.emp_id JOIN departments d
  ON d.dept_id = e.dept_id

  WHERE b.balance_type = ''年假'' AND b.as_of_quarter = ''2025Q3'' GROUP BY d.dept_name
  ORDER BY 年假使用率 DESC LIMIT 3'
