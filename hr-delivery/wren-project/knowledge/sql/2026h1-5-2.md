nl: 2026H1人均月度人力成本（含社保）最高的前5个部门？
sql: 'SELECT dept_name AS 部门,

  round((sum(base_pay + overtime_pay + bonus) + sum(insurance_company)) / (count(DISTINCT
  emp_id) * 6.0), 0) AS 人均月成本

  FROM v_workforce_monthly WHERE pay_period BETWEEN ''2026-01'' AND ''2026-06''

  GROUP BY dept_name ORDER BY 人均月成本 DESC LIMIT 5'
